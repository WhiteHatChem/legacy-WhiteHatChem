A correlation appears to exist between the minimum common oral dosage and the maximum common oral dosage for all the substances listed on [psychonaut](https://psychonautwiki.org/) and [tripsit](https://drugs.tripsit.me/), for which the necessary information is available. This represents a total of 453 dosage pairs. The Pearson coefficient is equal to *0.98*. This suggests that the maximum common oral dose can be estimated from the minimum common oral dose and vice versa, with the formulas:

<div style="text-align: center"> *oral.dose.common.max=1.77 * oral.dose.common.min + 2.95* </div>
<div style="text-align: center"> *oral.dose.common.min= 0.56 * oral.dose.common.max - 1.67* </div>

**Be careful** with this simplistic generality, you can explore the many counter examples in the graph above. It is also possible that the data was entered with a similar rule in mind and therefore does not reflect any biological truth.
